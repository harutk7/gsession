About TransLogistX

Trusted logistics. Verified people. Protected freight.

TransLogistX is a logistics network connecting verified professional drivers with trusted cargo opportunities. We combine driver validity checks, guided KYC onboarding, wholesale distribution for leading brands, and cargo insurance support into a single accountable platform.

Our goal is simple: shippers should know exactly who is moving their freight, and drivers should work inside a network that values their credentials and protects the cargo they carry.

Verified people

Every driver on the network completes identity, license, and validity checks before receiving assignments.

Wholesale distribution

We source and move goods for leading consumer brands with dependable wholesale logistics support.

Protected freight

Eligible shipments are covered by cargo insurance support from pickup through final delivery.

Accountable operations

KYC onboarding and documented reviews keep every account and assignment traceable.
