Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Who we are

TransLogistX (“we”, “us”) operates a logistics network for verified drivers, wholesale distribution, and protected cargo. For data protection matters, TransLogistX acts as the controller of the personal data described in this policy.

Registered address and company registration number are available from TransLogistX dispatch on request.

2. Data we collect

When you submit a driver verification or KYC application, we collect: your full legal name, phone number, email address, driver license number, primary operating region, and the identity and license documents you upload.

We also collect standard technical data when you browse the site, such as log data and device information used for security and performance.

3. How we use your data

We use your data to verify your identity and driver validity, review and manage your KYC application, operate your account, match you with suitable cargo assignments, arrange cargo insurance support, and meet legal and regulatory obligations.

We do not sell your personal data.

4. Legal bases

We process your data based on your consent (given when you submit an application), the performance of a contract or steps toward one, our legitimate interests in running a safe logistics network, and compliance with legal obligations.

5. Sharing and processors

We share data only with service providers that help us operate the platform (for example secure hosting and storage providers), with insurers where needed to arrange cargo coverage, and with authorities where the law requires it. All processors are bound by contractual data-protection obligations.

6. Retention

We keep application and verification data for as long as your account is active and for the period required by applicable transport, tax, and compliance laws afterwards. When data is no longer needed, it is securely deleted.

7. Security

We apply technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including access controls and encrypted transmission. No method of transmission is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information to industry standards.

8. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or port your personal data, to object to processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

9. Contact for privacy requests

For any privacy question or to exercise your rights, contact our privacy team at privacy@translogistx.com. We aim to respond within 30 days.
