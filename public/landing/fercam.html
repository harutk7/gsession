Welcome to FERCAM

FERCAM: innovation and quality at the service of your Logistics

FERCAM is the strategic partner that transforms your logistics challenges into tailored solutions, designed to optimize every phase of your supply chain. With over 75 years of experience, cutting-edge technology, and an extensive presence through proprietary branches in Europe, Asia, and Africa, we offer comprehensive and innovative support, responding with agility and precision to the needs of every sector.

Our services encompass road and intermodal transport, sea and air freight, integrated logistics management, customs consulting, removals and relocation services, support for fairs and events, and niche markets such as art transport and hospitality services. In Contract Logistics we operate through locations in Italy, Spain, and the Netherlands, ensuring flawless coordination of your logistics activities.

We are actively and continuously committed to promoting sustainability by combining operational excellence with environmental and social responsibility, reducing the environmental impact of our operations every day.

Discover the sectors in which we specialise

Logistics

FERCAM offers the market integrated logistics solutions. Our experienced and highly specialised team offers dedicated advice to manage the complexity of your warehouse and transport logistics in every product sector. We develop customised and advanced projects dedicated to you and based on your specific needs, streamlining your supply chain and ensuring that it operates efficiently with optimal production costs and timeframes.

Find out more

Air & Sea

The reliable solution to get your business moving. Thanks to the network of reliable partners all over the world and highly qualified personnel, FERCAM is a reference in air and sea freight. Our highly qualified staff manages your shipments of goods to and from all ports and airports in the world thanks to their direct presence or through reliable partners worldwide.

Find out more

Transport

Overland and intermodal transport for national and international third parties. Based on your company's specific freight needs, FERCAM will assess your requirements and recommend the most suitable solution. Our Transport division offers various road and intermodal transport solutions for your goods, both in Italy and abroad.

Find out more

Distribution

For any type of shipment, in Italy and in Europe. For any type of shipment, be it small packages or groupage deliveries, FERCAM offers a range of services to meet your needs. Thanks to a widespread network of branches and qualified partners in Italy and abroad, you can count on the reliability of FERCAM for defined delivery times, with the certainty of always knowing where your goods are located.

Find out more

Special services

Specialist transport services for oversized goods, removals, works of art, materials for exhibitions and events. FERCAM offers a range of highly specialised services for the transport of delicate and oversized goods. Our dedicated team takes care of the planning and shipment of your goods down to the smallest detail, and ensures safe handling during every stage of transportation.
